5 good practices when creating a webpage:

  • Make sure the content is easy to read
  • Easy navigation
  • Make sure it is legible on any device
  • Keep it minimal without unnecessary clutter
  • Keep paragraphs short

5 bad practices when creating a webpage:

  • Lack of space
  • Inconsistent colors or design
  • Complicated navigation
  • Low-quality images
  • Long blocks of text
